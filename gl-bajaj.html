THE EDUCATION RESEARCH BUREAU

GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management (GLBITM), located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, stands as one of the premier self-financed engineering institutions affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU). Established in 2005, the institute has steadily built a reputation for academic discipline and aggressive campus placement drives, making it a focal point for B.Tech and management aspirants across Northern India.

1. Comprehensive Placement Records & Salary Packages

Campus placement performance remains GL Bajaj's strongest selling point. The institute operates a dedicated Training and Placement (T&P) Cell that initiates technical preparation, coding bootcamps, quantitative aptitude sessions, and mock technical interviews right from the fifth semester.

2. Academic Fee Structure & Financial Breakdown

Compared to deemed private universities in the National Capital Region (NCR), GL Bajaj offers a relatively controlled fee architecture mandated under AKTU regulations, supplemented by institute development charges.

Official academic fee structure sheet outlining tuition, registration, and development fees.

The standard tuition fee for B.Tech programs averages between INR 1,40,000 to INR 1,60,000 per annum, depending on admission category (UPTAC counseling vs management quota seats). In addition to tuition, students pay mandatory AKTU examination fees and one-time security deposits during first-year enrollment.

3. Campus Life, Discipline & Hostel Infrastructure

College life at GLBITM presents a unique mix of high academic rigor and structured routine. Unlike sprawling open-campus universities, GL Bajaj operates on a compact, highly utilized campus footprint designed around focused productivity.

Academic Environment & Strict Discipline

GL Bajaj is widely recognized for its high school-like academic discipline. Mandatory attendance criteria (minimum 75%) are strictly enforced. Regular class tests, mid-semester evaluations (Sessional Exams), and strict dress code policies define daily campus life. While this environment can feel intense, students acknowledge that it builds consistency for competitive coding and campus interview clearance.

Hostel Accommodation & Facilities

On-campus hostel accommodations are available separately for male and female students:

  • Room Configurations: Double and Triple sharing rooms equipped with individual study tables, chairs, wardrobes, and wooden beds.
  • Hostel Charges: On-campus non-AC hostel fees range approximately from INR 1,05,000 to INR 1,40,000 per annum (inclusive of boarding, mess meals, and Wi-Fi access).
  • Amenities: 24/7 power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi access in common areas, air-cooled rooms, gym, basketball/badminton courts, and round-the-clock security surveillance.
  • Off-Campus Accommodation (PGs): Due to limited hostel capacity, a significant portion of students opt for private PGs and rented apartments in nearby Knowledge Park II and Alpha 1 sectors, which offer flexible entry timings and lower costs.

Mess Food & Extracurricular Culture

The campus mess provides 4 meals a day (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner). Student reviews rate the mess quality as decent compared to peer institutions in Greater Noida. The college hosts annual technical fests, hackathons, cultural events, and sports tournaments, providing necessary recreation amid academic schedules.

Final Verdict

GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management remains a compelling choice for students whose top priority is securing a high-value placement offer in software engineering or IT services without paying exorbitant tuition fees. Its combination of structured training, top-tier recruiting partners, and proven 50+ LPA package records solidifies its standing among the top AKTU colleges in North India.
