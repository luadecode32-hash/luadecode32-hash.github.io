Gujarat Student Life & Campus Culture

Campus Life, Festivals & Culture Across Gujarat’s 4 Engineering Hubs

Gujarat offers one of India’s safest, most entrepreneurial student environments—with ultra-low state government tuition fees (~₹1,500/year for boys and ₹0 for girls!) and vibrant Navratri/cultural traditions across every campus.

1. Ahmedabad & Gandhinagar Twin-City Hub

Research, ICT & Startup Capital

Institutes: IIT Gandhinagar, DA-IICT, LDCE, Nirma University, PDEU, VGEC, IITRAM, Ahmedabad Univ., GEC Gandhinagar

  • Vibe & Culture: IIT Gandhinagar (400 acres on Sabarmati river) features 5-star GRIHA sustainable hostels, zero-ragging mentorship, and Blithchron / Amalthea fests.
  • ICT & Private Excellence: DA-IICT boasts a green ICT-focused campus (Synapse fest), while Nirma Univ. & PDEU offer lush SG Highway/Raisan campuses with active clubs.

2. Vadodara (Baroda) & Charotar Education Belt

Heritage & Core Industry

Institutes: MSU Baroda (FTE), BVM Vallabh Vidyanagar, DDU Nadiad, CHARUSAT, ADIT, GCET, Parul University

  • Vibe & Culture: MSU Baroda (Kalabhavan) and BVM Vidyanagar (Est. 1948) are steeped in 75+ years of engineering heritage, FootPrints fest, and strong core manufacturing/chemical hiring.
  • Student Town Life: Vallabh Vidyanagar & Nadiad (DDU) operate as dedicated university towns with low living costs and tight-knit peer communities.

3. Surat & South Gujarat Industrial Corridor

NIT Legacy & Core/Tech Mix

Institutes: SVNIT Surat, IIIT Surat, GEC Surat, SCET Surat (Sarvajanik)

  • Vibe & Culture: SVNIT Surat (250 acres in Ichchhanath) sits in prime Surat with vibrant Sparsh & MindBend fests, great food culture, and strong PSU + software hiring.
  • Emerging Tech: IIIT Surat shares strong academic/industry ties with SVNIT and Gujarat’s diamond/textile/IT ecosystem.

4. Rajkot & Saurashtra Hub

Affordable & Engineering Hub

Institutes: GEC Rajkot, Marwadi University

  • Vibe & Culture: GEC Rajkot offers ultra-subsidized government engineering education in Saurashtra’s industrial heartland, while Marwadi University provides modern NAAC A+ residential facilities.

How B.Tech / B.E. Admissions Work in Gujarat (2026 Counselling Guide)

1. ACPC Gujarat Counselling (GUJCET + Boards)

ACPC prepares merit using 50% Class 12 PCM Theory Percentile + 50% GUJCET Percentile for Gujarat Board/Domicile seats in LDCE, MSU Baroda, VGEC, GECs (95% seats) and 50% State Quota seats in DA-IICT, Nirma, PDEU, BVM, DDU.

2. All-India Quota via JEE Main (No Domicile Needed!)

Top private & PPP universities in Gujarat reserve 35% to 50% of seats for All-India JEE Main CRL applicants through their own portals: DA-IICT (50%), Nirma Univ. (35%), PDEU (50%), and Ahmedabad Univ.—open to students from any state!

3. JoSAA / CSAB Counselling

Covers Central & PPP institutes in Gujarat: IIT Gandhinagar (JEE Advanced), SVNIT Surat (JEE Main: 50% Gujarat HS / 50% OS), and IIIT Vadodara & IIIT Surat (JEE Main CRL).
