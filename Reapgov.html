All REAP Government B.Tech Colleges in Rajasthan: Rank, Real Fees & Placements

Complete preference-wise list of all state government engineering colleges, autonomous societies, and state university departments participating in REAP (Rajasthan Engineering Admission Process) via JEE Main and Class 12 merit.

How Rajasthan Govt. Engineering Fees Work (GAS vs. SFS):
• GAS (Government Aided Seats) / TFWS: Subsidized seats costing roughly ₹60,000 – ₹1.40 Lakh for the entire 4-year degree (even lower for Girls & SC/ST).
• SFS (Self-Financed Seats): Most CSE/IT and general seats in Rajasthan govt. colleges fall under SFS, costing roughly ₹2.20 – ₹3.70 Lakh total for 4 years (excluding optional hostel/mess).

Key REAP Counselling & Reality Notes:
1. MNIT Jaipur & IIT Jodhpur Excluded: Centrally funded institutes (MNIT Jaipur, IIT Jodhpur, IIIT Kota) do not participate in REAP; they admit students exclusively via JoSAA/CSAB.
2. Out-of-State Students: 15% of seats in REAP government colleges are open to candidates outside Rajasthan based purely on JEE Main rank.
3. Best ROI Picks: MBM Jodhpur (especially CSE, IT, Mining, Electrical), CTAE Udaipur (CSE, Mining, Agriculture Engg.), and RTU Kota (CSE, Petrochemical) have the strongest alumni networks and PSU/core recruitment among Rajasthan state colleges.
