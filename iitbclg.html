4 Key Takeaways from IIT Bombay’s 5-Year Placement Data

1. Median CTC Held Firm Even During the 2023–24 Tech Dip

While overall placement percentage dipped from 89.3% (2022) to 74.5% (2024) due to global tech layoffs, IIT Bombay’s median CTC actually rose from ₹16.0 LPA to ₹17.92 LPA—proving that lower-paying mass IT roles were replaced by high-value core R&D, semiconductor, and quant roles.

2. Core Branches (Mech, Aero, Chem, MEMS) Saw Strong 2025 Rebound

Driven by India’s EV, aerospace, semiconductor (ISM), and manufacturing push, core departments saw placement percentages jump by 6–10 percentage points in 2024–25 alongside steady ₹17–21 LPA average packages.

3. Why "Unplaced" at IITB Doesn't Mean Unemployed

Roughly 12–15% of non-placed registered students at IIT Bombay opt out late in the cycle for MS/Ph.D. admits at MIT, Stanford, CMU, ETH Zurich, UPSC civil services, or venture-backed startups incubated at SINE IIT Bombay.

4. B.Tech vs. M.Tech / Ph.D. Divergence

Overall PT Cell figures combine undergraduate and postgraduate batches. When isolated, B.Tech & Dual Degree programs consistently achieve 88%–94% placement rates with a B.Tech-only average CTC exceeding ₹26.5 LPA.

Methodology & Data Note: Overall annual figures (2020–21 through 2023–24) reflect official IIT Bombay Placement & Training (PT) Cell annual placement reports, while 2024–25 reflects Phase-I & Phase-II consolidated placement outcomes. Branch-wise figures combine B.Tech + Dual Degree (5-year) departmental statistics to represent true undergraduate/integrated cohort performance.
