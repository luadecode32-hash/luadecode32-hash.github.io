Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about B.Tech average packages and college selection.

Which B.Tech college has the highest average package in India?

As per this directory, IIIT Hyderabad has the highest mean average B.Tech package at about ₹31.5 LPA, followed by IIIT Lucknow (~₹30.5 LPA), IIIT Bangalore (~₹28.5 LPA) and IIT Kanpur (~₹26.9 LPA).

What is the average package of IIT Delhi B.Tech?

The overall mean average B.Tech placement package at IIT Delhi is approximately ₹21.5 LPA across all branches. CSE and IT branches typically report higher averages than the institute-wide mean.

Are these average packages mean or median?

All figures in this directory are Mean Average CTC (LPA) taken from institutional placement reports — not median. Mean averages are usually pulled upward by a small number of very high packages, so the median is often lower.

Which state has the best B.Tech colleges in India?

Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana and Delhi NCT together host the largest concentration of high-package B.Tech institutes. IIT Bombay (Maharashtra) at ~₹23.5 LPA and IIT Madras (Tamil Nadu) at ~₹22.0 LPA lead their respective states.

What is the average package of NIT Trichy?

NIT Tiruchirappalli (NIT Trichy) reports an overall mean average B.Tech package of approximately ₹17.8 LPA — one of the highest among all NITs in India.

Which is the best private B.Tech college in Maharashtra by package?

Among state-affiliated private colleges in Maharashtra, Sardar Patel Institute of Technology (SPIT), Andheri leads with approximately ₹15.1 LPA, followed by PICT Pune (~₹12.8 LPA) and DJSCE Mumbai (~₹10.5 LPA).

Does a higher average package always mean a better college?

No. Average package is only one signal. Also consider branch-wise placement, median package, number of students placed, recruiter quality, fee structure, location, faculty, and admission cut-offs before deciding.
