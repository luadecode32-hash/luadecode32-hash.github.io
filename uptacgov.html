Reality Check & Counselling Tips:
1. Fee Breakdown: In UP Rajkiya Engineering Colleges (RECs), the official tuition fee is ~₹40,000/year plus ~₹12,300 user charges and ~₹7,500 AKTU university exam/registration fees (~₹60,000/year total, or ~₹2.2–₹2.4 Lakh for 4 years excluding hostel/mess).
2. UP Scholarship: Eligible UP domicile students with family income under ₹2 Lakh (Gen/OBC/Min) or ₹2.5 Lakh (SC/ST) get full tuition fee reimbursement via the UP Post-Matric Scholarship.
3. Branch vs. College: For CSE/IT aspirants, IET Lucknow, KNIT Sultanpur, BIET Jhansi, and Lucknow University have strong on-campus tech placements. In older RECs (Sonbhadra, Banda, Ambedkar Nagar, Bijnor, Azamgarh), core/mass IT recruiters (TCS, Wipro, Infosys, HCL) and GATE preparation drive most outcomes.
